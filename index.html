HOW IT WORKS

From estimate to finished floor in 3 simple steps

In-Home Estimate & Moisture Testing

We visit your location with real wood species samples, stain swatches, and subfloor moisture tools. The estimate is 100% free.

Board Acclimation & Laser Leveling

Boards acclimate to your interior climate while the subfloor is laser-calibrated for a flat, squeak-free, long-lasting install.

Artisan Joinery & Dustless Finish

Master crews install and finish with HEPA dust containment and protective multi-coat Bona low-VOC polyurethane.
