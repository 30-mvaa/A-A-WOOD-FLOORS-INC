THE WORKFLOW

From consultation to finished floor in 3 clear steps

1. In-Home Inspection & Moisture Testing

We visit your site with physical wood samples, stain swatches, and electronic subfloor moisture meters. Our comprehensive quote is 100% free.

2. Acclimation & Laser Leveling

Boards acclimate to your interior climate while subfloors are laser-leveled and secured with acoustic underlayment for a silent, stable floor.

3. Artisan Joinery & HEPA Finish

Master crews execute every joint with tight tolerances, vacuuming continuously with HEPA units and sealing with commercial low-VOC Bona coatings.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about your flooring project

Clear, honest answers to help you plan your hardwood installation, dustless sanding, or vinyl flooring investment.

Dustless sanding utilizes two-stage industrial vacuum units equipped with certified 99.97% HEPA filters connected directly to the sanding equipment. This captures airborne wood dust at the point of contact, keeping your air clean, protecting furniture, and preventing duct contamination.
Solid hardwood generally requires 5 to 7 days to acclimate to your interior temperature and relative humidity. Engineered flooring typically acclimates in 48 to 72 hours. We always measure subfloor and wood moisture levels with calibrated pin meters prior to beginning installation.
Yes. We use premium commercial-grade waterborne finishes (such as Bona Traffic HD) that are GREENGUARD Gold certified for low chemical emissions. They cure rapidly, emit virtually zero odor, and provide outstanding resistance to pet claws and daily wear.
